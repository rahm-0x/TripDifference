Change an account's attribution

Attribution is fixed at signup. This moves an account to another code — at that scout's current rate — and records who did it and why. Earnings already written stay where they are.

Change who introduced a scout

An introduction is fixed at approval. This moves a scout to another master — at that master's current override rate — or removes it, and records who did it and why. Overrides already written stay with the master who earned them.
