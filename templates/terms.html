1. What we do

We book flights for your business. After a ticket is issued, we watch its fare, and when the same flight becomes cheaper, we can exchange your ticket for the cheaper fare and get the difference back for you. We charge a fee only on money we actually recover by rebooking.

  • An exchange moves the traveler to the same flight at a lower fare. We exchange a ticket either when someone on your account confirms it, or automatically when the exchange returns at least a minimum amount to you and costs you nothing. We never automatically make an exchange that you would have to pay for.

5. Refunds and airline credit

When a ticket is exchanged or cancelled, the airline decides how any money comes back:

  • Cash. The airline refunds us, and we refund that amount to the card you paid with.
  • Airline credit in the traveler's name. It's subject to the airline's rules, including expiry dates and who can use it, and it can't be turned into cash or moved to another person.
  • Nothing. Some fares return nothing on a change or cancellation.

We show you what the airline is offering before you confirm.
