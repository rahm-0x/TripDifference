Everything after booking runs without you.

Built to be checkable

Every decision has a reason attached, and you can read all of them.

Priced by the airline

A cheaper seat on the board is not a cheaper exchange. We act on the exchange quote, which already nets out the change penalty.

Nothing to chase

No alerts to act on and no forms to file. The exchange happens as one operation, so you are never between two tickets.

No saving, no fee

Our share comes out of what we recover. If the exchange never turns in your favour, there is nothing to charge you for.

Same flight, or nothing

We only accept an exchange onto the identical carrier and flight numbers. A cheaper reroute through a connection is not a saving.

Unwatchable fares are flagged

Some fares can never be exchanged, and some carry a change fee too large to beat. We say so before you book, not after.

An auditable trail

Every check writes a line: what the market showed, what the exchange quoted, which threshold applied and what we decided.

Stop paying yesterday's price

Book the flight you were going to book anyway. We will keep checking whether the airline will give some of it back.
